The rules
What the extractor may and may not do with a plan, so nothing invented ends up looking like a reservation.

Never marked booked. Everything from a pasted plan lands as "to book". Only an email with a confirmation number can create a real booking.

No invented prices. A plan has no amount, no confirmation code, no cancellation deadline. Those fields stay empty until you actually book.

Times are taken, not guessed. If a row has no time it becomes an undated item on that day rather than being slotted somewhere plausible.

You confirm first. Same review sheet as the inbox scan — nothing is written until you say so.

One more thing this unlocks. Once a "to book" item exists with a date, the plan check can watch it for you: a timed-entry attraction still unbooked a week out, two things overlapping at 15:45, or a dinner reservation on a night you're in a different city. That's the same rules engine you already have — it just gets something new to look at.
