# How to report back

Ashley is not an engineer and reads these answers on a phone as often as a
laptop. Long explanations bury the one thing she has to do next. So:

- Lead with **what you did or are about to do**, then **what you need from
  her** as a numbered list of actions, in order. Keep them visually separate.
- Recommend one path. Don't lay out options she didn't ask to choose between.
- Say what you couldn't verify, in a line — never bury a caveat in prose.
- Explain the *why* only when it changes a decision she has to make, and then
  in a sentence or two, ideally with an example or analogy rather than
  terminology.
